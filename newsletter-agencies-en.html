Dear travel partner,

We are pleased to introduce Rainforest Resorts, the Costa Rican resort family bringing together Chachagua Rainforest Resort & Hot Springs and Arenal Rainforest Resort. For your agency, this means two sister properties to consider when shaping a Costa Rica itinerary, each with its own accommodation and setting.

You know your clients: their interests, expectations and the way they like to travel. Our local Travel Designers support your itinerary with accommodation options, experience planning and the practical details to confirm before you quote. Recommend one resort or explore a stay across both; the itinerary follows your clients’ needs.

Below you will find ten experience ideas and selected photographs for your own monthly newsletter or an edition every two months. Download the images, choose the story that fits your audience and keep your agency’s contact details as the call to action. Your agency manages its own mailing list; no client-list upload or sharing is requested.

10 experiences to discuss with your clients

Choose according to interests, ages, mobility and time in the itinerary. The first five take place at Chachagua; for guests staying at Arenal they require a separately coordinated visit to the sister property. The other five are external excursions from either resort.

Location: Chachagua

A simple editorial plan

For a monthly edition, introduce the resort family first, then feature one accommodation and two or three experiences. For an edition every two months, develop one itinerary theme in more detail. Add your agency’s advice and contact information, and direct enquiries back to your team.

Arenal: information for your proposals

Renewal is taking place in stages, with construction or remodelling continuing during 2027. Noise or changes to areas and services may occur. Before presenting a final proposal, confirm the category or unit, current photographs, expected work and operating services for the travel dates. Do not describe the entire property as fully renovated or promise a volcano view from every room.
