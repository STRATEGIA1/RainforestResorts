Dear travel partner,

We are pleased to introduce Rainforest Resorts, the Costa Rican resort family bringing together Chachagua Rainforest Resort & Hot Springs and Arenal Rainforest Resort. For your agency, this means two sister properties to consider when shaping a Costa Rica itinerary, each with its own accommodation and setting.

You know your clients: their interests, expectations and the way they like to travel. Our local Travel Designers support your itinerary with accommodation options, experience planning and the practical details to confirm before you quote. Recommend one resort or explore a stay across both; the itinerary follows your clients’ needs.

Below you will find ten experience ideas and selected photographs for your own monthly newsletter or an edition every two months. Download the images, choose the story that fits your audience and keep your agency’s contact details as the call to action. Your agency manages its own mailing list; no client-list upload or sharing is requested.

Two properties to recommend

Match the stay to the traveller

Chachagua Rainforest Resort & Hot Springs

For couples. Recommend the Deluxe Bungalow or the Jungle Tree House for time together immersed in the forest.

For small groups. Explore the Rainforest Bungalow, River Villa and Casita Suite; match the room layout and occupancy to your clients.

For larger groups and families. Consider the Two Bedroom Villa, Hacienda Cercone and La Fortuna Country House for shared space and privacy. Our Travel Designers can help you choose the property and location, confirm capacity and review current photographs before you quote.

Explore Chachagua stays

Arenal Rainforest Resort

Explore One Bedroom Vista Arenal and Bungalow Arenal as distinct accommodation options for your clients’ Arenal itinerary.

Recently acquired by its new owners, Arenal Rainforest Resort is beginning a new chapter. Throughout 2026, the team has been working on renovating the first eight units, with further improvements continuing during 2027.

As we work to bring the resort to its best, coordinate with our Travel Designers to select the accommodation best suited to your guests. They will help you review the specific unit, current photographs, operating services and work expected for the travel dates. Renovation may bring construction noise or changes to some areas and services.

Your itinerary shortlist

10 experiences to discuss with your clients

Choose according to interests, ages, mobility and time in the itinerary. The first five take place at Chachagua; for guests staying at Arenal they require a separately coordinated visit to the sister property. The other five are external excursions from either resort.

01

Daily Nature Walk

Location: Chachagua

Follow the forest with a guide, noticing plants, sounds and the small details that shape this living landscape.

Location: Chachagua
