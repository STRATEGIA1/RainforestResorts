12 experiences for your clients’ Costa Rica journey

Dear travel partner,

From guided rainforest walks to coffee and cacao and adventures around Arenal, these twelve selected experiences help you shape your clients’ Costa Rica itinerary.

Whether your clients stay at Chachagua Rainforest Resort & Hot Springs or Arenal Rainforest Resort, our Travel Designers work with your agency to match experiences to their interests and coordinate reservations and transport from the selected resort.



Our editorial selection

Choose the experiences that suit your clients

Choose the experiences you would love to include. Our Travel Designers can recommend the same carefully selected excursions from either resort and help you plan the route, reservations and transport from the place you choose to stay.

The first four experiences take place at Chachagua. For Arenal guests, they are outings to the sister property with access, transport, costs and participation confirmed separately. For horseback riding and excursions beyond the resort, our team will confirm the meeting point, route and transport for your stay.
