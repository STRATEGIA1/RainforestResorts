Launch copy audit · Costa Rica your way

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Scope: the 24 organic posts, four bilingual articles, eight snippets and newsletter in the November–December proposal. English Instagram and Facebook captions were counted separately; mentions in proposal chrome were not counted as post copy.

Finding

The original plan had enough total content but insufficient explicit launch coverage. Chachagua named Rainforest Resorts in 0/12 Instagram captions and 0/12 Facebook captions. Arenal named it in 0/12 Instagram captions and 1/12 Facebook captions. Cross-property activities alone do not explain the new umbrella brand or sister-property relationship. B01 and B04 mentioned the umbrella in their bodies; B02 and B03 did not. No blog title introduced the new family. The only combined-stay article was dated 21 December, and the newsletter was dated 23 December: too late for an announcement in the next few weeks.

Four launch-led touchpoints per brand are an editorial recommendation, not a platform rule or a forecast of reach. The revised sequence establishes the relationship in early November, explains shared experiences and service later in November, and reinforces it in December. Each selected post names Rainforest Resorts and connects both properties; the remaining eight posts per resort retain product and experience storytelling. This improves message coverage but cannot establish audience frequency without actual delivery data.

Brand architecture

Rainforest Resorts is the Costa Rican resort family. Chachagua Rainforest Resort & Hot Springs is the familiar rainforest property and sender of the launch newsletter. Arenal Rainforest Resort is the sister property being introduced; it is undergoing progressive renewal, not presented as a newly built or fully renovated resort. A Travel Designer is a local adviser who helps select accommodation, experiences and logistics. Each property retains its official name and logo. No new Rainforest Resorts logo is invented.

Newsletter design and audience

The newsletter is the first section below the proposal introduction and the first navigation item. It has a Rainforest Resorts masthead, explicit launch lead, the two official resort logos, two accommodation modules, 12 editorial experience cards with real locations, a blog module, Arenal renovation information and property-specific Travel Designer CTAs. Full English and Spanish editions remain separate.

Audience assumption: the authorised Chachagua subscriber base, using guest-facing language as requested. The previous version addressed trade partners; list segmentation and actual send date must be confirmed before sending. No commercial rates, commissions or invented benefits are announced. Sender details, postal address, preferences and working unsubscribe must be supplied in the email platform.

The selection is editorial, not a claim that these are objectively the ten best activities. All twelve are listed in Chachagua’s current official catalogue. The shared website catalogue is a future alignment supplied by the client; it is not evidence that all experiences already operate at both sites or are included in every booking. Chachagua activities require separate access and transport coordination for Arenal guests. Off-site activities have separate logistics from each resort. No times, durations, minimum ages or included transport are copied across properties.

Accommodation and source checks

  • Chachagua accommodation: the newsletter names Two Bedroom Villa, Rainforest Bungalow, Jungle Tree House, River Villa and Casita Suite; private residences are kept distinct.
  • Arenal accommodation: checked directly in the browser. Bungalow Arenal and One Bedroom Vista Arenal are listed; the site also explicitly identifies Chachagua as its sister property.
  • Chachagua experiences: all 12 newsletter links point to their current Chachagua detail pages while catalogue alignment is pending.
  • Client’s launch brief, version 1.3 dated 30 October 2026: primary source for the family name, story, shared-catalogue plan and ongoing 2027 renewal.

The Chachagua accommodation index still uses Master Bungalow, while its detail URL redirects to Deluxe Bungalow. This naming inconsistency needs reservation-team confirmation; it is not silently renamed in existing category-specific posts or photographs. The newsletter avoids featuring that disputed label. Existing accommodation-image assignments and operational details still require final client approval.
