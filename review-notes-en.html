What is included

Four full articles, each in English and Spanish; 24 bilingual organic posts; eight additional bilingual blog snippets; one newsletter in separate EN and ES versions; 20 paid concepts adapted into 60 market-specific variants. The proposed dates are not publishing actions.

Language and brand

English first, then three dashes and Spanish for organic captions. US and European paid ads are English only; Costa Rica ads are Spanish only. STRATEGIA uses its official full-colour logo and Poppins. Hotel creative uses the supplied Chachagua and Arenal logos, their forest/ivory/terracotta/wine/olive palette, Morion and Messina Sans. Market and audience proposal

US$6,000 across both hotels and both months. Each hotel: US$1,500 monthly, split as US$900 United States (60%), US$375 UK European pilot (25%), and US$225 Costa Rica (15%). Paid creative personas: couples 50%, friends 30%, families 20%; no solo targeting or creative. These are hypotheses for review, not measured market or booking shares. Reassess using qualified enquiries and booking outcomes.

Booking windows

November content proposes January–March 2027 stays; December proposes February–April 2027. These are broad editorial windows, not measured lead times or weather guarantees. Revise using origin-market and reservation data. First posts rely on existing approved assets because November has already begun.
